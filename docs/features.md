# Features

### 🔋 Energy Management Module
- **Real-time Consumption Tracking:** Monitor power usage across individual devices and aggregate room/house totals.
- **Automated Savings:** Rules to power down high-drain devices during peak tariff hours or when a room is unoccupied.
- **Historical Analysis:** Visualize energy trends over days, weeks, or months to identify inefficiencies.

### 🛡️ Security System
- **Intrusion Detection:** Correlate data from door/window sensors and motion detectors to trigger alarms.
- **Camera Integration:** View live RTSP feeds and receive snapshot alerts when anomalous activity is detected.
- **Presence Simulation:** Automatically toggle lights and media devices to simulate occupancy when the house is armed in "away" mode.

### 🧠 AI-Based Automation
- **Rule-Based Engine:** Create complex logic sequences (e.g., "If motion detected AND time is after sunset AND lux is < 50, turn on hallway lights").
- **Predictive Behavior (ML):** The system learns typical occupancy patterns and preferred temperature settings to proactively adjust the environment.
- **Smart Notifications:** Context-aware alerts that prioritize critical events (e.g., water leak) over routine updates (e.g., vacuum finished).
